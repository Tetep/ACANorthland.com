What This Is

Brainpower, Banter & a Great Night Out

Third Thursday Trivia is ACA Liberty's monthly evening event for anyone who wants to put their knowledge to the test in a great setting. We take over the 1894 Restoration Building on Liberty Square — doors open at six, trivia kicks off at six-thirty.

Grab your team of two to six, show up ready, and expect a mix of categories that'll keep everyone in it. Whether you dominate or get humbled, you'll leave having had a genuinely good time. It's become one of the most looked-forward-to nights on the Liberty Square calendar.

Good to Know

How the Night Works

  • Doors open at 6:00 PM — get there early to grab a good spot
  • Trivia begins at 6:30 PM sharp
  • Teams of 2 to 6 players — come solo and we'll get you on a team
  • Multiple rounds, mixed categories — general knowledge, local, pop culture
  • 1894 Restoration Building, second floor — free street parking on Liberty Square
  • ACA members and non-members welcome
