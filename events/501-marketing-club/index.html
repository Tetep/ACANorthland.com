What This Is

Practical Marketing. Real Connections. Smarter Growth.

The 5:01 Marketing Club is a monthly evening gathering for Northland professionals who want to get better at growing their business — not just talk about it. We meet the first Thursday of every month at ACA KC North on Vivion Road, starting at 5:01 on the dot.

Each month features a guest speaker or facilitated discussion around a specific marketing topic — things you can actually use. Lead generation, local SEO, content that converts, referral systems. No fluff, no pitches. Just practical knowledge shared among people who are building something.
