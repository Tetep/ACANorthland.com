What This Is

Bid, Breakfast & Have a Blast

Quartermania is a fast-paced, quarter-bidding event where everyone walks away with something — and nobody needs a big budget to play. Your $10 entry gets you three bidding paddles, a hot breakfast, and a seat at the table. Then the fun starts.

Items go up for bid one at a time. You bid in quarters — keep it rolling, keep it casual. It's part yard sale, part game show, and completely open to the public. Bring a friend, bring your quarters, and come ready to have a good time on Liberty Square.

Good to Know

What's Included & What to Bring

  • $10 entry fee at the door — no pre-registration required
  • 3 bidding paddles included with admission
  • Breakfast included — hot and ready when doors open
  • Bring quarters — the more you have, the more you can bid
  • Open to the public — members and non-members welcome
  • Free street parking on Liberty Square
