What This Is

A Monthly Evening for the Whole Northland Network

Starting in June, ACA Northland is hosting a monthly evening networking event at the new Liberty location — the 1894 Restoration Building on Liberty Square. Members from across the Kansas City chapter network are invited. Easy in, easy out: doors at 6, wrap by 7.

The kickoff in June is intentionally casual — beer, brats, and a cornhole board if the weather holds. It's a low-friction way to meet members you don't see at the morning coffees, and a first look at the Liberty space for those who haven't been by yet.

Members in good standing are welcome. Bringing a guest? One per member, vouched-in.
